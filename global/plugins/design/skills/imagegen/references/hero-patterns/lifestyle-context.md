# Lifestyle context hero

The product or service shown in real, aspirational use — a person mid-experience, environment doing half the storytelling. E-commerce, hospitality, fitness, food delivery, consumer apps. Sells the *outcome feeling*, not the object.

## What the layout does to the image

- Full-bleed with copy in a quiet zone, or a large split-column image.
- The scene implies the customer's better life; casting (age, style, setting) IS the brand positioning — decide who the aspirational customer is before prompting.
- Often paired with a product-object shot elsewhere on the page; the lifestyle hero carries emotion, the product shot carries detail.

## Image requirements

- A specific moment, not a pose: "pouring the first coffee of the morning", "lacing up before a trail run at sunrise" — verbs make it candid, adjectives make it stock.
- The product/service visible but *in use*, secondary to the human moment; generic enough to avoid label/text artifacts (blank packaging, unbranded gear).
- Environment with named time-of-day light: golden hour, soft morning window light, warm dusk — lifestyle lives on light quality.
- Editorial camera language: 35–50mm, f/2–f/2.8, natural film grade ("Kodak Portra warmth").
- If copy overlays: apply the quiet-zone technique from `full-bleed-text-left` (named calm medium on one side).

## Aspect ratio

`16:9` full-bleed; `4:3` or `3:4` for columns.

## Prompt recipe

[moment as a verb phrase] + [specific person, wardrobe, expression] + [product in use, unbranded] + [environment + time-of-day light] + [camera: 35–50mm, wide aperture, film grade] + [optional quiet zone clause] + [no text].

## Example prompts

Outdoor gear (full-bleed, text left):

> Editorial lifestyle photograph, wide composition. The left half of the frame is soft pre-dawn sky over a misty valley, smooth muted blue-grey gradient with no objects. On the right, a hiker in a rust-orange shell jacket kneels on a rocky overlook pouring steaming coffee from a steel flask into a small cup, headlamp glowing faintly, gear unbranded. First cold light of sunrise rims the ridge behind her. Shot at 35mm f/2.8, gentle film grain, Kodak Portra warmth in the highlights. Quiet, aspirational, real. No text, no letters, no logos.

Meal-kit / food delivery (split column):

> Warm candid photograph, 4:3 composition filling the frame. A father and daughter cook together at a bright kitchen island, mid-laugh as she tips chopped vegetables from a wooden board into a sizzling pan he steadies, fresh ingredients scattered naturally across the counter, plain kraft-paper packaging visible but unmarked. Golden early-evening window light from the left, shallow depth with the kitchen melting into warm bokeh that fills every corner. 50mm f/2, editorial not stock, genuine expressions. No text, no letters, no logos.

## Pitfalls

- Casting vagueness produces stock-photo people; specify age, wardrobe, and what the hands are doing.
- Branded products invite garbled logos — keep gear/packaging plain and composite real branding later if needed.
- Multiple simultaneous "moments" (four people, four activities) dilutes the story: one moment, one or two people.
- Check hands (holding, pouring, gripping) at full size — interaction poses are where anatomy fails.
