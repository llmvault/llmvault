# Human portrait hero

One person, close and real, as the hero's emotional anchor — shallow depth of field, authentic expression, environment melted into bokeh. For businesses selling trust and human connection: coaching, healthcare, recruiting, local services, personal brands, professional services.

## What the layout does to the image

- Usually a split layout (portrait in the image column) or full-bleed with copy beside the subject's quiet side.
- The subject's gaze is a layout element: eyes looking toward the copy pull the reader to the headline; eyes to camera create direct connection. Choose deliberately.
- Faces attract the eye before anything else on the page — the portrait must be flawless, because every viewer will inspect it.

## Image requirements

- ONE subject with a specific identity: age range, role, wardrobe, expression, what they're holding/doing. Specificity is what separates editorial from stock.
- Shallow depth of field: "85mm lens at f/1.8, face tack sharp, background melting into creamy bokeh" — the verified formula for premium portrait feel.
- The environment visible in the bokeh should tell the brand's story (clinic, workshop, kitchen, site) without competing.
- Genuine expression language: "warm genuine smile", "quiet focused confidence", "mid-laugh" — not "smiling professionally".
- Gaze/body direction chosen for the layout (see above).
- Authentic texture: skin detail, work-worn gear, natural asymmetry. Avoid "perfect model" language, which produces uncanny stock.

## Aspect ratio

`3:4` or `1:1` for split columns (portrait orientation flatters faces); `16:9` only for full-bleed with the subject in one third.

## Prompt recipe

[close portrait of + specific person: age, role, wardrobe] + [expression] + [what they hold/do] + [gaze direction relative to the frame] + [85mm f/1.8 formula] + [environment described as bokeh] + [light source] + [mood words] + [no text].

## Example prompts

Trades / construction services:

> Close portrait of a construction site engineer, mid-thirties, warm genuine smile, wearing a scuffed yellow hard hat and hi-vis vest, holding a rolled blueprint over his shoulder, gaze directed just past the left of the frame. Shot at 85mm f/1.8 — face tack sharp, the construction site behind him melting into creamy golden bokeh with hints of cranes and scaffolding. Late afternoon sun as a soft key light, authentic skin texture, trustworthy and personal. No text, no letters, no logos.

Healthcare / therapy practice:

> Close portrait of a physical therapist in her fifties, silver-streaked hair tied back, calm open expression with the beginning of a smile, arms loosely crossed in a sage-green tunic, looking directly into the camera. Shot at 85mm f/2 — eyes perfectly sharp, a bright airy clinic dissolving into soft white-and-wood bokeh behind her with a hint of green plants. Diffused window light from the right, honest skin texture, warm and reassuring. No text, no letters, no logos.

## Pitfalls

- Inspect hands and teeth at full size on every result — they are the model's weakest anatomy and an instant reject.
- Gaze pointing off the page (away from the copy) leaks attention; re-prompt the gaze direction explicitly.
- Generated faces imply a fictional person — never present one as a real staff member or use where authenticity is legally/ethically required (testimonials, team pages). Flag this to the user when relevant.
