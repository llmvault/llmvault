# Isometric scene hero

A detailed isometric diorama as the hero visual — a miniature world viewed at the classic 30° angle. Suits technical, logistics, infrastructure, and platform products: it *explains* while it decorates, showing many parts of a system in one image.

## What the layout does to the image

- Almost always placed in a split layout's image column or floating over a plain/gradient background — isometric art rarely works as an overlay background.
- The diorama's plain backdrop is often matched to the section background color so the scene appears to float on the page deliberately.
- Readers study isometric scenes — small narrative details (tiny workers, vehicles, arrows of movement) reward attention and communicate product scope.

## Image requirements

- One coherent "platform" or scene block, viewed at a consistent 30° isometric angle, no vanishing-point perspective.
- Accept the style's centered-diorama gravity (see the main skill's levers): the scene WILL sit as a floating island on plain background. Either match that backdrop color to the page, or demand corner-to-corner coverage explicitly if a full-bleed look is needed.
- Rich but organized detail: distinct zones (loading area, building, road) rather than uniform clutter.
- Consistent light direction with soft shadows; a harmonious 4–6 color palette.
- Tiny human figures and vehicles add life and scale.

## Aspect ratio

`4:3` or `1:1` for columns; `16:9` only if the scene is explicitly panoramic ("a long isometric strip of connected buildings").

## Prompt recipe

"Detailed isometric vector illustration of [scene], viewed at a 30-degree isometric angle" + [the platform/ground element] + [3–4 named zones with specific contents] + [tiny figures/vehicles and what they're doing] + [palette] + [backdrop instruction: either a named flat backdrop color, or "the scene spans the whole frame corner to corner"] + [no text].

## Example prompts

Logistics platform (floating diorama, backdrop matched to page):

> Detailed isometric vector illustration viewed at a 30-degree isometric angle: a compact logistics hub on a rounded rectangular platform. A warehouse with a partially transparent roof revealing shelving inside, two loading docks with a truck backing in, a small yard with stacked shipping containers in coral and navy, a delivery van departing along a curved road, tiny workers with pallet jacks. Soft shadows, single warm light direction, clean precise linework. Palette: warm cream platform, navy, amber, terracotta, mint accents, on a flat pale sage backdrop. No text, no letters, no logos.

Construction site (frame-filling variant):

> Detailed isometric vector illustration of an active construction site viewed at a 30-degree isometric angle, the scene spanning the whole frame corner to corner with no plain backdrop visible. A partially built mid-rise with exposed floors, a yellow tower crane lifting a beam, scaffolding with ladders, a cement mixer truck on a site road, tiny isometric workers in orange vests moving between stacked pallets and steel piles that reach into the frame corners. Soft shadows, harmonious palette of navy, amber, terracotta and mint. Premium, precise, organized detail. No text, no letters, no logos.

## Pitfalls

- Fighting the floating-island tendency half-heartedly gives you a scene that *almost* fills the frame — worse than either committing to the diorama or forcing full coverage.
- Mixed projection (isometric buildings + perspective vehicles) looks broken; repeat "isometric" for the scene as a whole.
- Too many zones turns the diorama into noise: 3–4 zones maximum, each with one activity.
