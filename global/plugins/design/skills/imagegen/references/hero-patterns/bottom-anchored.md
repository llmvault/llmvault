# Bottom-anchored hero

Copy (headline, subhead, CTA) sits in the upper half of the section; the image's visual interest runs along the bottom — a skyline, a crowd, a product row, a landscape ridge. The top of the image is quiet sky/atmosphere.

## What the layout does to the image

- Text occupies the upper 40–55%, usually centered or left-aligned, over the image's sky area.
- The bottom band (subjects) is fully visible and often slightly darkened by a bottom gradient for depth.
- Responsive cropping trims the sides; the bottom band must read even when only the central portion shows.

## Image requirements

- A horizontal band of subject matter in the lower 30–40% of the frame, interesting across its full width.
- The upper 55–65% is one continuous atmospheric field: gradient sky, soft clouds, haze. Uniform enough for overlaid text.
- Tone contrast between zones: light sky + dark text, or dusk sky + white text. Pick one and prompt for it.
- Skylines, treelines, mountain ridges, rows of people/products photographed from a low angle all work naturally.

## Aspect ratio

`16:9`. For full-viewport heroes `3:2` gives more sky to play with.

## Prompt recipe

[low horizon / low-angle scene] + [what forms the bottom band, spanning the full width] + [named sky/atmosphere filling the upper two thirds] + [lighting, grade] + [no text].

## Example prompts

Construction / engineering firm:

> Wide photograph with a deliberately low horizon line. Along the bottom quarter of the frame, a construction skyline spans the full width: cranes, steel frameworks, and silhouetted workers on a girder, backlit at dusk. The upper three quarters of the image is an enormous smooth gradient sky — deep indigo at the top blending into warm amber at the horizon, a few thin distant clouds, no objects. Epic, calm, monumental mood. Cinematic grade, slightly dark exposure. No text, no letters, no logos.

Music festival / events:

> Photograph from within a festival crowd at night, low camera angle. The bottom third of the frame is a sea of raised hands and silhouetted heads spanning edge to edge, lit by warm stage glow and drifting haze. The upper two thirds is deep night sky filled with soft violet and magenta light beams dissolving into darkness — atmospheric, no objects, no lens flare in the upper area. Energetic yet dreamy, grain like high-ISO concert film. No faces in focus, no text, no letters, no logos.

## Pitfalls

- If the horizon lands mid-frame, the text zone shrinks: "low horizon line" and "the upper two thirds of the image is sky" are the load-bearing phrases.
- Clouds with hard bright edges behind text kill contrast; ask for "smooth gradient sky" or "soft haze".
- Crowd scenes generate faces — keep them silhouetted/backlit to avoid mangled-face rejects.
