# Full-bleed hero, text right

Mirror of `full-bleed-text-left`: edge-to-edge image, copy block in the right third to half, subject anchored left. Chosen when the page's visual rhythm, logo placement, or the subject's natural direction favors a left-side subject.

## What the layout does to the image

- Copy is right-aligned or left-aligned inside a container occupying the right 40–50%.
- A right-to-left scrim may be applied; the right side must tolerate darkening (or be light enough for dark text).
- RTL-language sites and pages with left-anchored navigation drawers often prefer this orientation.

## Image requirements

- All visual weight in the left 50–60% of the frame.
- The right 40–50% is one continuous low-detail field (sky, wall, water, bokeh) with consistent tone.
- Subject facing or gesturing *rightward*, into the copy zone, so the viewer's eye lands on the headline.

## Aspect ratio

`16:9` (or `3:2` for tall heroes; CSS crops vertically).

## Prompt recipe

Identical logic to `full-bleed-text-left`, mirrored: [subject in the left third, facing right] + [named quiet medium filling the right half] + [lighting/mood] + [grade] + [no text]. Never mention text, layouts, or websites.

## Example prompts

Healthcare / wellness:

> Warm editorial photograph, wide composition. In the left third of the frame, a physiotherapist guides an older patient through a gentle stretching exercise in a bright clinic, both facing toward the right of the frame, natural genuine expressions. The right half of the image is a smooth wall of soft morning light and pale sage green, completely plain and evenly lit, with a faint out-of-focus plant shadow at the far edge. Airy, hopeful, human. 50mm lens at f/2.8, gentle film grain. No text, no letters, no logos.

Logistics / delivery:

> Cinematic photograph at golden hour. A courier on a cargo e-bike rides through the left third of the frame, leaning into motion toward the right, slight motion blur on the wheels, warm sunlight catching the rider's jacket. The right half of the frame is open empty street bathed in soft golden haze fading into bright bokeh — no vehicles, no signage, just light. Teal-shadow amber-highlight grade, energetic but clean. No text, no letters, no logos.

## Pitfalls

- Same page-mockup risk as the left-text variant — no layout vocabulary.
- Motion subjects must move INTO the quiet zone, not out of the frame; "moving toward the right of the frame" is the load-bearing phrase.
- Watch for signage/storefront text artifacts in street scenes; the "no letters" clause matters most here.
