# Product / object hero

A single physical product as the star: floating on a clean field, standing on a simple surface, or caught mid-motion with dynamic elements. E-commerce, hardware, CPG, fashion accessories. The image sells the object's materiality — texture, weight, finish.

## What the layout does to the image

- Split layout (product right, copy left) or centered product under/over a centered headline.
- The product's background field often blends into the section background — choose and name that backdrop color deliberately.
- The product will also be reused at other sizes (cards, thumbnails), so a clean silhouette pays repeatedly.
- If true transparency is needed, generate on a clean solid field for cutout (see the transparent/cutout guidance in the main skill).

## Image requirements

- ONE product, described with obsessive material specificity: "brushed aluminum body", "matte stoneware glaze with speckle", "full-grain tan leather with visible pores". Material language is what makes objects feel real.
- Studio lighting named explicitly: "soft directional key light from the upper left, gentle gradient falloff, soft contact shadow beneath". A contact shadow (or reflection) grounds the object; floating without one looks pasted.
- Simple named backdrop: one color field or minimal surface ("pale warm grey seamless backdrop", "honed concrete slab"). No props unless each is named.
- Camera angle chosen for the product: three-quarter for dimensional objects, straight-on for bottles/boxes, slight top-down for flat goods.
- Generous margin around the product on all sides — this is the one pattern where the subject should NOT touch the frame edges.

## Aspect ratio

`1:1` or `4:3` for split/centered layouts; `3:4` for tall products (bottles).

## Prompt recipe

[product with brand-neutral material description] + [angle] + [backdrop field, named color] + [studio lighting + contact shadow] + [optional single dynamic element: splash, drifting steam, one falling ingredient] + [generous empty margin around the product] + [no text on packaging or anywhere].

## Example prompts

Headphones (hardware):

> Studio product photograph of premium over-ear headphones in matte charcoal with brushed aluminum yokes and soft pebbled leather ear cushions, three-quarter angle, floating a few centimeters above a pale warm-grey seamless backdrop with a soft contact shadow directly below. Soft directional key light from the upper left creating gentle highlights along the aluminum edges, subtle gradient falloff in the background, generous empty margin around the product on all sides. Crisp material detail, high-end and minimal. No text, no letters, no logos anywhere.

Skincare (CPG, with one dynamic element):

> Studio product photograph of a frosted glass skincare bottle with a bamboo cap, standing on a wet slate pedestal against a deep sage-green backdrop field. A single slow ripple of water spreads from the pedestal's base, and one green eucalyptus sprig lies beside it — no other props. Soft diffused light from above with a gentle rim light from behind, soft reflection under the bottle, generous margin around the composition. Calm, premium, spa-like. Blank frosted glass with no label, no text, no letters, no logos.

## Pitfalls

- Any real brand name or logo in the prompt gets mangled into pseudo-text on the product — describe materials and shape, keep packaging blank, composite real labels afterward in editing.
- More than one hero product per image splits attention and invites duplication artifacts.
- Skipping the contact shadow is the #1 "looks fake" cause for floating products.
