# Split breakout hero

An asymmetric split (commonly 55/45 or 60/40) where the image column bleeds off the right viewport edge — cropped by the browser window itself. Conveys energy, scale, and momentum. Popular with product companies, mobility, travel, and bold SaaS rebrands.

## What the layout does to the image

- The image's right side is cut by the viewport: whatever sits at the right edge gets amputated on smaller screens.
- The left edge of the image borders the text column, often with generous gap — the image's left region should lead the eye back toward the copy.
- The crop is *part of the design*: subjects intentionally extend past the frame, implying the world continues.

## Image requirements

- Subject anchored in the LEFT-to-CENTER of the image, fully intact there; expendable environment extending rightward.
- Something deliberately croppable on the right: a vehicle's tail, a building's far wing, a landscape continuing — content that reads naturally when sliced.
- Directional composition pointing left (into the page) — motion, gaze, or leading lines.
- Full-bleed scene, no margins.

## Aspect ratio

`3:2` or `16:9` — wider than the visible column, since the right portion exists to be cropped.

## Prompt recipe

[subject in the left half of the frame, complete and detailed] + [environment continuing and extending toward the right edge of the frame] + [leftward-facing direction] + [style/lighting] + [no text].

## Example prompts

Electric vehicle / mobility:

> Dynamic photograph, wide 3:2 composition. A sleek electric delivery van sits in the left half of the frame, complete and crisply detailed, angled so its front faces the lower left corner, dramatic side lighting at dusk. The van's body and the wet street it stands on continue and extend past the right edge of the frame, trailing reflections and city bokeh that run off the image. Cool blue evening palette with warm orange headlight accents, cinematic grade. No visible license plate, no text, no letters, no logos.

Architecture / construction firm (illustration):

> Textured editorial illustration in a risograph print style filling the whole canvas. In the left half of the frame, two bold silhouetted construction workers guide a steel beam, rendered in navy and warm orange with visible print grain, complete and centered in that half. The beam and a lattice of scaffolding extend rightward, running off the right edge of the frame as if the site continues beyond the image. Cream paper texture showing through, dynamic diagonal energy pointing back toward the left. No text, no letters, no logos.

## Pitfalls

- If the subject drifts to the image center it gets cropped on real screens — "in the left half of the frame, complete" is load-bearing.
- Right-facing subjects lead the eye off the page; keep gaze/motion pointed left.
- Test the mental crop: cover the right 30% of the generated image — if it still composes, it works.
