# Pitch Decks

Investor pitch decks must work without a presenter. They need denser, clearer slide copy than live talks, and every slide should help a reviewer understand the business quickly.

## Pitch Deck vs Presented Deck

| Aspect | Pitch deck | Presented deck |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| Text density | Higher; must stand alone | Lower; speaker adds context |
| Structure | Expected investor categories | Flexible narrative |
| Traction | Prominent and parseable | Can be discussed verbally |
| Ask | Explicit dedicated slide | Can build naturally |
| Length | 10-15 slides max | Depends on setting |
| Reading test | Forwardable with no context | Clear in a few seconds |

## The 10-Slide Framework

### 1. Title

Company name, one-line description, contact info, and optional traction hook.

### 2. Problem

Who has the pain, why it matters now, and what it costs. Lead with customer language or data when available.

### 3. Solution

Product in 30 seconds. Show before/after or workflow change instead of a feature list.

### 4. Traction

Revenue, users, growth rate, retention, milestones, logos, or usage proof. Move this earlier if the numbers are strong.

### 5. Market Size

TAM, SAM, SOM, and why now. Prefer bottom-up logic over broad market claims.

### 6. Business Model

Revenue streams, pricing, unit economics, sales motion, gross margin, CAC, LTV, or payback period.

### 7. Competition

Landscape matrix, quadrant, or buyer criteria. Never claim there is no competition.

### 8. Team

Names, roles, relevant credentials, founder-market fit, and why this team can win.

### 9. Financials and Roadmap

Realistic projections, key milestones, hiring, product roadmap, and use-of-funds preview.

### 10. The Ask

Amount raising, round, use of funds, milestones it unlocks, runway, and clear next step.

```text
Raising: $XM [stage]
Use of funds: product, GTM, operations
Milestone unlocked: [specific business milestone]
Next step: [clear CTA]
```

## Writing for Async Review

- Headlines must be complete: "1,000+ customers, $10M ARR" beats "Traction".
- Use two to three bullets per section, each a complete thought.
- Put metrics in selectable text where possible, not only inside images.
- Define acronyms on first use.
- Use charts over tables, tables over bullets, bullets over paragraphs.
- Pass the forwardable test: if an investor forwards this with no context, does the slide still make sense?

## Investor Screening Optimization

Investors and their teams may skim or use tools to extract deck facts. Make key facts easy to parse:

- Clear slide titles matching expected categories.
- Metrics next to labels and dates.
- Consistent formatting for revenue, customers, growth, retention, and ask.
- Sources or assumptions for market sizing.
- Appendix slides for deeper diligence.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| No clear ask | Add explicit amount, use, runway, and next step |
| Features over benefits | Lead with customer outcome |
| Vague market size | Use bottom-up calculations |
| Missing traction proof | Add metrics, logos, testimonials, or usage evidence |
| Unrealistic projections | Tie assumptions to motion and evidence |
| Too many slides | Keep core deck to 10-15 and move detail to appendix |

## Canvas Format

- Use 16:9 Canvas presentation slides.
- Keep the deck readable at 50% zoom.
- Use selected Canvas brand colors, type, and logo rules.
- Include source labels for metrics and claims.
- Sync the final Canvas presentation artifact only after visual QA.
