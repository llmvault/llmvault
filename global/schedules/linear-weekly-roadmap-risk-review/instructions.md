# Linear Weekly Roadmap Risk Review

Review connected Linear teams, projects, cycles, and issues for roadmap risk.

Look for work that is slipping, blocked, unassigned, missing priority, stale in progress, missing estimates, near due date with low progress, or no longer aligned with current milestones. Check projects, cycles, labels, comments, and recent status changes when available.

Focus on risks that a product or engineering lead can act on this week. Do not move issues, change priorities, or update project status unless explicitly authorized.

Your report should include:

- Projects or cycles at risk.
- Blocked or stale issues needing owner attention.
- Scope or priority ambiguity.
- Missing dependencies or decisions.
- Recommended next actions and likely owners.
