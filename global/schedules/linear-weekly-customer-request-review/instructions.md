# Linear Weekly Customer Request Review

Review Linear customer-linked work and prepare a customer follow-up summary.

Look for customer needs, customer requests, support-linked issues, blocked customer-facing bugs, stale customer commitments, and issues where status has changed but follow-up may be missing. If the workspace does not use Linear customer objects, infer customer relevance from labels, projects, comments, and issue text.

Group requests by customer impact, urgency, product area, owner, and status. Do not update Linear records, change priorities, or send customer messages unless explicitly authorized.

Your report should include:

- Customer-impacting issues needing follow-up.
- Requests blocked on product or engineering decisions.
- Recently resolved items that may need customer notification.
- Repeated customer themes.
- Suggested owner actions for the next week.
